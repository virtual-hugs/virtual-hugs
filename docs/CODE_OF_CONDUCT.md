# 💙 Virtual Hugs Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Virtual Hugs is a collaborative project built around kindness, creativity, learning, and inclusivity.

We are committed to creating a welcoming and respectful environment where everyone — regardless of experience level, background, or skill set — feels comfortable contributing, asking questions, sharing ideas, and learning new things.

Whether you are an experienced developer or making your first GitHub contribution, you are a valued member of this community.

---

# 🌱 Expected Behavior

All contributors are expected to:

- Treat everyone with kindness and respect.
- Be patient and supportive toward beginners.
- Encourage others to ask questions.
- Give constructive feedback instead of harsh criticism.
- Respect different ideas, opinions, and approaches.
- Communicate honestly about progress, challenges, and availability.
- Give credit to others for their contributions.
- Collaborate and help create a positive learning environment.

Remember: everyone is here to learn and build something meaningful together.

---

# 💬 Communication Guidelines

When communicating with other contributors:

✅ Ask questions when you need help.  
✅ Explain your ideas clearly and respectfully.  
✅ Provide helpful feedback.  
✅ Assume positive intentions.  
✅ Be open to learning from others.

Avoid:

❌ Making others feel bad for being beginners.  
❌ Dismissing someone's ideas without explanation.  
❌ Using disrespectful or insulting language.  
❌ Creating an environment where people are afraid to participate.

---

# 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or bullying of any kind.
- Discrimination based on someone's identity or background.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Intentionally disrupting the project or community.
- Sharing inappropriate or harmful content.
- Taking credit for someone else's work.
- Repeatedly ignoring community guidelines after being reminded.

---

# 🤝 Collaboration Expectations

Virtual Hugs is a team project. Contributors should:

- Communicate if they are unable to complete a task.
- Respect other contributors' time and effort.
- Ask before making major changes that affect other people's work.
- Be willing to receive and provide feedback.
- Help maintain a positive and supportive atmosphere.

Mistakes are a normal part of learning. We encourage contributors to learn from mistakes rather than fear making them.

---

# 🌟 Beginner-Friendly Environment

Virtual Hugs welcomes contributors of all experience levels.

Please remember:

- Everyone starts somewhere.
- Asking questions is encouraged.
- Learning new skills takes time.
- No one is expected to know everything.

Experienced contributors are encouraged to support newer contributors and help them grow.

---

# 📢 Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project lead.

Reports can be made through:
- Direct message to the project lead
- Private communication channel provided by the team

All concerns will be taken seriously and handled respectfully.

---

# 💙 Our Goal

Virtual Hugs is not only about creating a website. It is about creating a community where people can learn, collaborate, and make something that helps others.

Thank you for helping make this project a kind, creative, and welcoming space.
